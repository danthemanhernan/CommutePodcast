## Suggested lab

Perform the five-boundary audit from the episode on one workflow in a portfolio repository:

1. Classify triggers by trust level.
2. Trace untrusted data into commands and paths.
3. Record permissions and secret access by job.
4. Trace caches, artifacts, packages, and other cross-job outputs.
5. Identify safe parallelization candidates and their shared resources.
